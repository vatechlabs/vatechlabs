Who we are

What information you may provide

If you choose to contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, you may provide your name, email address, company name, phone number, selected service, and details of your request. Please do not send passwords, medical records, financial identifiers or other sensitive information through a general enquiry.

How the enquiry form works

Our website prepares an email draft in your own email application (or lets you download an enquiry text file). The website itself does not transmit the enquiry to our servers or store form submissions. Information is transmitted when you decide to send the email, and the email provider you use processes it under its own terms.

Why we use contact information

When you contact us, we use the information to respond, assess project requests, communicate about our services, maintain business correspondence, and comply with applicable legal duties. We do not claim that submitting the form enrolls you in marketing messages. Any future newsletter or promotional processing should have its own appropriate notice and choice.

Sharing and outside services

We may share relevant correspondence with personnel or service providers who assist with communication and service delivery, subject to appropriate protections, or disclose information where legally required. Email, telephone, WhatsApp, maps and hosting services may process data under their own privacy practices. Following an external link leaves our website.

Retention and security

Enquiries that you actually send may remain in our business mailboxes or records for as long as reasonably necessary for responding, managing our relationship, resolving disputes, or meeting applicable obligations. We take reasonable steps to safeguard business correspondence, but no electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your privacy choices and requests

You may contact us at info@vatechlabs.com to ask about personal information held in our communications, request correction or deletion where applicable, withdraw relevant consent, or raise a privacy grievance. We will assess requests under applicable law and any valid retention obligations. Indian digital-data-protection obligations have phased commencement dates, and this policy will be updated as applicable requirements take effect.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law, practices or services. The updated date on this page will identify revisions. Material changes should be communicated where required.
